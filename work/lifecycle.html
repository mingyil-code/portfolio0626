What I did

Built the Klaviyo lifecycle programme across 7 APAC markets from a standing start: sign-up and activation, welcome, cart and browse abandonment, post-purchase and winback flows, each with behavioural segmentation. HubSpot stayed the CRM for contacts and pipeline, with reporting syncs the sales team adopted.

The biggest decision came before any of that went live: what to do with the contact list inherited from HubSpot.

The Klaviyo fix

When migrating the HubSpot database into Klaviyo, business contacts, customers and long-inactive contacts were all mixed into one list. Sending to it as-is would have damaged sender reputation from the first campaign. Before the first send, I built segments separating recent buyers and engaged contacts from everyone else, suppressed about 40% of the list as inactive, and sent only to the engaged audience. I also set up sending domain authentication and edited the HTML/CSS of the email templates directly. Bounce rate stayed under 0.8% from the first send, and click rate settled at 2.4%.
