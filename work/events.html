What I did

Marketed ticketed workshops, product events and conventions ranging from 30 to 2,000+ participants. For the 2023 North American Convention in Las Vegas, I spotted stronger-than-expected ticket momentum and proposed raising the target from 1,000–1,500 towards 2,000, then coordinated feasibility with the production team. It closed at about 2,000 participants. Ran pre-event promotion (early-bird offers, ticket bundles, participation incentives) and bilingual English-Chinese communications, tracked registrations on the e-commerce platform, and built dashboards on geography, member mix and bundle preferences that showed where to push next.

Beyond the brief

International delegates kept asking the same travel, accommodation and registration questions. I created a bilingual English-Chinese participant guide covering logistics end to end, which cut repetitive enquiries. On site, I handled registration, check-in, VIP coordination and international delegations; afterwards, follow-up communications, feedback collection and sales-impact analysis.
